The Back Office a Small
Business Never Had.

A large company runs on departments — operations, finance, sales, delivery, intelligence. A small business runs on one person covering all five, usually after the work is done. This is a staff that absorbs what repeats, so the owner keeps the parts that need a person: judgment, approvals, relationships, and signatures.

The roster

Five Departments.
One Owner.

Each seat covers work an owner is doing by hand right now. What it gives back is time, and a level of consistency that does not depend on how the week went.

01

Operations

runs the owner’s day
Daily Debrief

A morning brief from live data: where the business stands, what is due, what is at risk.

StopsThe morning scramble, and follow-ups remembered too late.

Chief of Staff

A short daily check-in and a Friday scorecard against the priorities you set, with drift flags when the week went elsewhere.

StopsGoals slipping for a month before anyone notices.

Watchdog

Monitors dead automations, failed form submissions and quotas running out, and posts a heartbeat so silence is never mistaken for health.

StopsFinding a week-long outage by accident.

Task Warden

Tracks overdue work, nags, and escalates whatever has gone stale.

StopsChasing your own team by text.

Sync Secretary

Builds the meeting agenda from open items and logs the decisions afterwards.

StopsPrepping meetings, then losing what was decided in them.

Watches the inbox, classifies receipts, files them, and keeps the ledger current.

Money Brief

A weekly money picture: spend by vendor, burn, collected, recurring revenue, runway.

StopsNot knowing the number until the accountant tells you.

Usage Auditor

Reviews cost and usage per tool, flags waste, and recommends plan changes.

StopsQuietly overpaying across a dozen subscriptions.

Collections Desk

Turns failed payments into drafted follow-ups waiting for your approval.

StopsPutting off the awkward money conversation.

Tax Vault

Quarterly set-aside math, filing clocks, and a package your CPA can actually use.

StopsTax-season panic.

Speed-to-Lead

StopsLosing the lead to whoever called back first.

Cadence Runner

Socials Suite

StopsPaying a content freelancer — or posting nothing for three weeks.

Review Sentinel

StopsReputation drifting because nobody replied.

Researcher

One sharp, cited question a week with three recommended actions.

StopsGuessing about competitors and pricing.

Proposal Forge

Turns lead details into a personalised, branded proposal automatically.

Affiliate Desk

Applications, vetting, onboarding and commission math for referral partners.
